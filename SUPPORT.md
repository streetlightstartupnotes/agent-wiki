# Support / 支持

Agent wiki is maintained by 路灯同学创业笔记.

Contact the publisher through [X](https://x.com/LDstartupnotes) or [GitHub](https://github.com/streetlightstartupnotes). Include the plugin version, operating system, the steps leading to the problem and a sanitized error message. Do not send your knowledge library, private conversations, tokens or runtime configuration.

反馈时请说明版本、系统、操作步骤和脱敏后的错误。不要公开私人知识库、对话原文、访问令牌或运行配置。请先备份正式笔记，再复现写入问题。
